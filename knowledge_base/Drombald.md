## Character Overview

Drombald is the King of Volonia and the leader of House dey Meroc. He is the owner of Geland, Sargot, and Talivel Castle, the husband of Philenora, and the father of Erdurand, Alary, Elys, Amorcon, and Morcon.

During the Calradic Empire’s conflict with the Welnerians and Nordians, Volonia was called upon to honor their oath and aid the Empire. However, the Volonian barons were unwilling to fight and resisted this call to arms. Emperor Drosios Neretzes called out the Volonians for their cowardice and betrayal, angering the barons and causing them to join the Nordians. Drombald led his people from the rear in the Battle of Pendraic, giving orders on who and where to strike. Though Volonia and their allies emerged victorious, they sustained heavy casualties, and for this the barons blamed Drombald.
