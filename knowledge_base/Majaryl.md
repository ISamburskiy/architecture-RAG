## Settlement Overview

Majaryl is a town in the Wester Conclave, owned by Emperor Gioras of the Comnos clan. The villages of Aegosca and Dradios are bound to the town.

Every realm has a city whose inhabitants are mocked for their provincial unsophistication — and for the Empire, that city was Majaryl. One of the first imperial colonies established inland of the Perassic Sea, it served as a base to subdue the western Palaic tribes. While Paravenos gladly accepted the plunder and slaves sent from Majaryl’ garrison, its elites quietly ridiculed the town’s people.

That perception shifted when the Jalmaryan general Carsos staged a military coup. In a now‑infamous moment, a senator, flustered by Carsos’ thick accent, nervously asked him to repeat a decree. In response, Carsos unsheathed his sword and said: “Do you understand this?”

Given the similar names and location, Majaryl is presumably the predecessor of Halmar — a town of the Khergit Khanate depicted 173 years later in *Mount & Blade: Warband*.
