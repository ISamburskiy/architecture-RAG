## Settlement Overview

Marthunal, known as the “Horse of the Ford,” is a town in Welneria ruled by High King Crafendil of the fen Gruffendoc clan. Despite the name, the town is nowhere near a river. The name stems from a legendary tale favoured by Welnerian bards: the hero Ochlaigan, who was foretold to defeat the seven sons of Glanys in seven duels near seven fords, encountered his last enemy at Marthunal. To fulfil the prophecy, he slit his horse’s throat and crossed over the stream of blood that gushed forth, defeating his final foe.

- **Port:** No
