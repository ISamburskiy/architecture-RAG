## Character Overview

Enaricos Pethros was the last undisputed Emperor of the unified Calradic Empire and the former leader of the Pethros clan. He was the husband of Empress Ragea of the Southern Conclave and the father of Ira.

Elected by the Senate to succeed the unpopular Drosios Neretzes soon after the disastrous Battle of Pendraic, Enaricos used the rift between the Welnerians and Nordians to end the war through a series of successful military campaigns. Prior to ascending the throne, he served as one of Neretzes’ most trusted subordinates and commanders and enjoyed a competent and esteemed senatorial reputation. Viewed as the savior of the Empire, he even began to be worshipped by a cult as a partly divine ruler.

Enaricos’ military exploits and careful stewardship slowed the Imperial decline. However, he was assassinated before naming a successor, which triggered the fragmentation of the Empire and sparked the civil war between Conul Osticos, Gioras Comnos, and his widow Ragea.
