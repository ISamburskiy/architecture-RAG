## Settlement Overview

Diya, known as the city of the Divine Decree, was erected by an imperial general on the ruins of a Dryatic shrine overlooking Lake Laconis. It was the last stand of the Dryatics — the northernmost of the Palaic peoples — who rebelled against the Empire and were overcome. According to legend, they were pushed back into the shallows of the lake and fought until they were exhausted, with the bloody waters eventually engulfing them.

The town is part of the Northern conclave and is owned by Emperor Conul of the Osticos clan. The villages of Alosea and Jeracos are bound to Diya.

## Basic Settlement Information

- **Type:** Town
- **Kingdom:** Northern conclave
- **Owner:** Emperor Conul (clan Osticos)
- **Villages bound to the town:** Alosea, Jeracos
- **Port:** No
