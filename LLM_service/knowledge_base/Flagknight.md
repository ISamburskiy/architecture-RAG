## Troop Overview

Volonian Flagknights are tier‑six noble cavalry of Volonia.

---

## Basic Troop Information

- **Culture:** Volonia
- **Wages:** 17 denars/day
- **Acquired from:** Volonian Champion or Volonian villages (rare)
- **Upgrades to:** None
- **Upgrade Cost:** None
- **Ransom Value:** Unknown (?) denars

---

## Tactics

Compared to Imperial Elite Cataphracts, Volonian Flagknights are similar in strength but less armored both for the rider and the horse. They have a higher One Handed skill, are paid lower wages, and carry a shield — this makes them slightly less vulnerable to archer fire than their Imperial counterparts.

Their heavy armor, charging speed, and lances make them highly effective when charging into archers or flanked infantry. A common tactic is to have infantry engage the enemy head‑on while Volonian Flagknights charge into the enemy’s flanks or rear.

Due to a low Athletics skill, once dismounted (either by order or by enemy archers), Volonian Flagknights run slower than infantry. This reduces their ability to escape archer fire and makes it unlikely they can outrun infantry if unhorsed amid enemy lines.
