## Troop Overview

Kerghit Keshiks are tier‑six noble horse archers of the Kerghit.

---

## Tactics

Despite their name suggesting a guard role, Kerghit Keshiks are among the most versatile and effective troops in the game. They are equipped with a bow, two stacks of arrows, a powerful two‑handed polearm, top‑tier armor, and an armored warhorse.

They excel as horse archers and skirmishers, but their true strength lies in melee combat with their glaive. In equal‑numbers fights on even terrain, they can consistently outperform Volonian Flagknights and Imperial Elite Cataphracts — even when ordered to hold fire. When dismounted and ordered to hold fire, they can rival Welnerian Veteran Falxmen, Nordian Heroic Berserks, and even Imperial Legionaries in melee.

As foot archers, they are surpassed only by Welnerian Flinger Champions. Unlike the Welnerian nobles, who are primarily archers and only moderately effective as shock troops, Kerghit Keshiks can be deployed in multiple roles: sieges, skirmishing, shock attacks, stationary archery, infantry reinforcement, or holding off heavy cavalry.
