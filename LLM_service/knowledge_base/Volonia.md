## Kingdom of Volonia

### General Information
* **Capital City**: Geland
* **Ruling Monarch**: King Drombald
* **Ruling Clan**: dey Meroc
* **Minor Factions**: Brotherhood of the Woods, Company of the Golden Boar
* **Territory Location**: Volonia region

### Overview
The Volonians originated from early warlord **Wilund the Bold** (Calradic: "Valandion"). They began as a coalition of barbarian tribes who migrated into former Welnerian lands during the decline of the Calradic Empire.

**Key Characteristics**:
* Specialize in powerful, well-armored heavy cavalry units
* Field well-trained crossbowmen
* Cultural bonus: leaders gain 5% more renown from battles
* Mercenaries earn 15% more money
* Villages bound to castles are 15% more productive
* 20% higher influence cost to invite lords to armies
* Start with slightly more territory than other factions
* Vulnerable to internal dissent

### History
The Volonians were initially employed by the Calradic Empire for siege operations, utilizing their skills in crossbows and board shields. They were primarily paid in land grants and titles along the western coast.

Under **King Osric Iron-Arm**, the Volonians declared independence and established their own kingdom. He recognized his lieutenants as barons and counts, granting them lands in exchange for military support.

### Battle of Pendraic
* **King Drombald** ascended to power as a great-grandson of Osric Iron-Arm
* Empire called Volonians to war against Nordians and Welnerians
* Volonians suffered heavy casualties against Imperial cataphracts
* Battle considered a loss by many Volonian lords
