## High Kingdom of Welneria

### General Information
* **Capital City**: Marthunal
* **Ruling Monarch**: High King Crafendil
* **Ruling Clan**: fen Gruffendoc
* **Minor Factions**: Wolfskins
* **Territory Location**: Welneria region
* **Faction Color**: Green

### Overview
Welnerian civilization consists of loosely organized clans who originally populated most of the Calradian continent. Now they occupy heavily forested lands in the northwest region of Calradia.

**Specializations**:
* Forest warfare tactics
* Longbowmen units
* Two-handed swordsmen
* 50% reduced speed penalty when moving through forests

### History
The Welnerians are the original inhabitants of much of the Calradian continent. Their hilltop fortresses have witnessed countless wars against various invaders:
* Calradic Empire legions
* Nordian kingdom
* Volonian kingdom

**Key Characteristics**:
* Masters of longbow usage
* Experts in night raids
* Specialize in sudden wild charges from the woods
* Valor and mischief are highly idolized

### Tactics
**Strengths**:
* Powerful archers in noble troop line
* Falxmen with long-range weapons
* High-quality weapons and large shields
* Defensible territory

**Weaknesses**:
* Inferior cavalry options
* Limited ranged capabilities outside noble branch
* Some troops less well-armored
* Surrounded by four factions
* Weak economy
