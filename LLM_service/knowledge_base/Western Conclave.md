## Wester Conclave

### General Information
* **Faction Culture**: Calradic Empire
* **Capital City**: Majaryl
* **Ruling Monarch**: Emperor Gioras Comnos
* **Ruling Clan**: Comnos
* **Minor Factions**: Eleftheroi, Embers of the Flame, Hidden Hand, Legion of the Betrayed
* **Territory Location**: Western Calradic Empire

### Overview
The Wester Conclave is ruled by **Emperor Gioras**, a war hero and military populist who believes the Army should choose the Emperor. The Empire shares borders with:
* Welneria
* Volonia
* Northern conclave
* Southern Conclave

The Wester Conclave was formed after the death of Emperor Enaricos, when the Imperial Legions under the command of General Gioras proclaimed him to be the new emperor. With the financial backing of Apys Varros, Gioras plans to ensure the loyalty of his army veterans through generous land distribution plans.

### History
According to tradition, the first Calradians entered the continent as exiles from lands across the western ocean. The Empire's center of gravity moved east and south, and their original heartland has been lost to the Volonians. The western region is a warm green land of oak forests and volcanic outcrops, mostly conquered from the Palaic tribes. Service in the legions has traditionally been a path out of rural poverty.
