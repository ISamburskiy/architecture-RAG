## Character Overview

Conul Osticos is the Emperor of the Northern conclave, leader of the Osticos clan, and a claimant to the imperial diadem of the Calradic Empire.

Before the events of *Bannerlord*, Conul was serving as a junior officer in the staff of Emperor Drosios Neretzes. Following the defeat at Pendraic, he became a member of the Calradic Senate and gained great influence. When Neretzes’ successor, Enaricos Pethros, was suspiciously murdered, Conul became the leader of a group of powerful senators who claimed the Senate should take the role of ruling the Empire. Conul’s claim is based on the old Imperial law that the Senate should elect the person for the role.

Ragea Pethros and Gioras Comnos each contested this claim, leading to the three‑way Calradian civil war.
