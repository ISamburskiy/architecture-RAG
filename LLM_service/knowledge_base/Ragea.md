## Character Overview

Ragea Pethros is the Empress of the Southern Conclave, leader of the Pethros clan, standing alongside Conul Osticos and Gioras Comnos as one of the three pretenders to the throne of Calradia. Despite the fact that she leads a noble clan of the Empire, she was born a daughter to the Darshi Shah.

Before the events of *Bannerlord*, Ragea was the wife of the late Emperor Enaricos, who was suspiciously murdered. As his widow and mother to his daughter Ira, who is now heir‑apparent, Ragea claims it is the right of the Emperor’s family to inherit the Empire, and some Imperial citizens have also chosen to support her in the belief that a hereditary succession is what the Empire needs.

It is worth noting that, despite her age, Ragea’s widowhood only enhanced her position—and not only in politics. She soon became the most sought‑after woman on the entire continent. Suitors from every realm relentlessly attempt to win her favor, yet she has never tired of dismissing them, remaining resolute and unmoved by their advances.

She is one of the nobles who can provide information about the Battle of Pendraic for the quest *Investigate “Neretzes’ Folly”*.
