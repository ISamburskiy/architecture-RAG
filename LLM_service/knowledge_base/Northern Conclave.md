## Northern conclave

### General Information
* **Faction Culture**: Calradic Empire
* **Capital City**: Diya
* **Ruling Monarch**: Emperor Conul Osticos
* **Ruling Clan**: Osticos
* **Minor Factions**: Eleftheroi, Embers of the Flame, Hidden Hand, Legion of the Betrayed
* **Territory Location**: Northern region

### Overview
The Northern conclave is ruled by **Emperor Conul**, an influential senator and aristocrat who seeks to restore the Senate's right to appoint the new Emperor. The Empire shares borders with:
* Welneria
* Nordia
* Kerghit
* Southern Conclave
* Wester Conclave

The Northern conclave represents the **oligarchic Senate**, which supports the supremacy of the Senate over the Emperor. The Senate believes that the Empire's decline is due to overly powerful Emperors who are not accountable to their electors.
