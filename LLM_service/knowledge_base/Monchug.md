## Character Overview

Monchug Urkhunait is the Khan of the Kerghit and the leader of the Urkhunait clan. He is the owner of Makebtau, the husband of Anat, and the father of Bortu, Chaghan, Alijin, and Bolat.

Monchug is the son of Khan Urkhun, the former leader of the Kerghit. After his father’s death, Monchug took his place as Khan and became the leader of the Kerghit.

He is a cruel and dishonorable person, yet also calculating and generous. He sometimes executes nobles he has captured and is carefree about diplomatic relations — he will only act if it benefits him. When voting, he ignores his personal preferences. As a generous ruler, he occasionally grants fiefs to his vassals.
