## Settlement Overview

Nasala is a town and presumed capital of the Algazel Sultanate, ruled by Sultan Alunqid of the Banu Hulyan clan. The port of Nasala serves as the gateway through which the grain from the Damar River flows to feed a hungry world. Historically, the Empire’s advances south of the Perassic Sea were often aimed at seizing this strategic port. Even after the legions retreated, Nasala remains one of the most cosmopolitan cities in Calradia, bustling with merchants from many lands seeking the lowest bulk prices.

---

## Basic Settlement Information

- **Type:** Town
- **Kingdom:** Algazel
- **Owner:** Sultan Alunqid (clan Banu Hulyan)
- **Villages bound to the town:** Mijayit, Hamoshawat, Jahasim, Nahlan
- **Port:** Yes

---

## Points of Interest

- **Town notables:** Found walking the streets; may offer quests and jobs.
- **Keep:** Residence of the nobles; includes a dungeon for prisoners.
- **Arena:** For combat practice, tournaments, and perk reassignment via the arena custodian.
- **Tavern:** Place to rest and eat; hub for hiring Wanderers and Mercenaries, interacting with Ransom Brokers, and playing the local board game.
- **Market:** For buying and selling food, equipment, horses, and other livestock.
- **Smithy:** Used to forge new weapons or smelt unwanted ones into smithing materials.
- **Criminal gangs:** May appear in areas like the Backstreet or Waterfront.
