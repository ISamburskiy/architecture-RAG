## Character Overview

Alunqid Banu Hulyan is the Sultan of the Algazel and the leader of the Banu Hulyan clan. He is one of the nobles who can talk about the Battle of Pendraic.

The current Sultan of Algazel is Alunqid. He wears his authority lightly, preferring to charm rather than to coerce, and always defers to the laws of the Banu Asera in his judgments. However, some say that Alunqid is remiss in his duty to administer justice, allowing the wealthiest clans — such as his own — to use bribes and clever legal arguments to oppress the others.
