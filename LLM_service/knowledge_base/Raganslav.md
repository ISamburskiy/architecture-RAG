## Character Overview

Raganslav Gundaroving is the Grand Prince of Nordia and the leader of clan Gundaroving. He is the owner of Balesk, Varcheg, Mazhadan Castle, and Nevyansk Castle, the husband of Asta, and the father of Simir, Mimir, and Valla.

In year 1077, then‑Prince Raganslav marched with his father, Grand Prince Vadinslav, alongside the Welnerians and Volonians to confront the Calradic Empire at Pendraic. In the battle that followed, Vadinslav was killed as the Nordians fought the main imperial force and overwhelmed their camp. However, Raganslav did not participate in the frontline — he had been ordered by his father to stay back.

Upon receiving word of his father’s death, Raganslav claimed the Empire’s Dragon Banner as his father’s trophy and thus his inheritance. Olek the Old refused, breaking the banner’s staff and throwing it at his Prince. Raganslav was further enraged by what he perceived as cowardice from High King Crafendil fen Gruffendoc for ambushing the imperials instead of fighting them directly.
