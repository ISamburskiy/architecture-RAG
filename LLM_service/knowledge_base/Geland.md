## Settlement Overview

Geland is the capital of Volonia and is owned by King Drombald of House dey Meroc. The town overlooks the wave‑beaten Biscan coast, taking in the catch from the local fishermen who venture far into the western ocean, as well as the wool from sheep raised in the green, fog‑covered hills nearby.

The current line of Volonian kings traces its roots to the clans that settled this region during Osric Iron‑Arm’s invasion. Given the similar names and location, Geland is presumably the precursor of the Rhodok town of Yalen, as seen 173 years later in *Mount & Blade: Warband*.

---

## Basic Settlement Information

- **Type:** Town
- **Kingdom:** Volonia
- **Owner:** King Drombald (House dey Meroc)
- **Villages bound to the town:** Furbec, Meroc, Nogrent
- **Port:** No
- **Siege equipment usable against the town:** Battering Ram, Ladders, Towers

---

## Points of Interest

- **Town notables:** Found walking through the streets; they may offer jobs for denars.
- **Keep:** Residence of the nobles; includes a dungeon for prisoners.
- **Arena:** Used for combat practice, tournaments, and perk reassignment via the arena custodian.
- **Tavern:** Place to rest, eat, and drink; also a hub for hiring Wanderers and Mercenaries, interacting with Ransom Brokers, and playing the local board game.
- **Market:** For buying and selling food, equipment, horses, and other livestock.
- **Smithy:** Where new weapons can be forged.
- **Criminal gangs:** May appear in areas like the Backstreet or Waterfront.
