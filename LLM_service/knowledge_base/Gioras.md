## Character Overview

Gioras Comnos is the Emperor of the Wester Conclave, the leader of the Comnos clan, and a claimant to the Imperial diadem of the Calradic Empire.

Before the events of *Bannerlord*, Gioras was a soldier in Emperor Neretzes’ legions. After a successful military career, he became a wildly popular general and began to publicly insist that the army, not incompetent aristocrats, should choose the rulers of the Empire. When Emperor Enaricos was later murdered under suspicious circumstances, a large assembly of Gioras’ soldiers gathered to proclaim him as the new Emperor.

Conul Osticos and Ragea Pethros each contested this claim, leading to the three‑way Calradian civil war.

Gioras also claimed that his uncle Pateor — a veteran soldier — had been beheaded by Conul after petitioning for veterans to be granted lands by the Senate.
