## Troop Overview

Welnerian Flinger Champions are tier‑six noble archers of Welneria.

---

## Tactics

Welnerian Flinger Champions are *Bannerlord*’s premier foot archers: despite their rarity, even a small force can decisively win battles. Their bows are powerful, accurate, and out‑range most (if not all) other archers in the game. Their armor is comparable to that of heavy infantry, and their two‑handed swords are among the best in the game. They are especially effective against the Empire, as most Imperial armies consist largely of lightly armored and poorly shielded footmen.

Deploy them on high ground or in front of your own troops so they can fire unimpeded. They will typically out‑range enemies, forcing them to advance or suffer heavy casualties. If threatened by enemy infantry or cavalry, provide them with support from the rest of your army. Although they can hold their own in melee due to an exceptionally high Two Handed skill, their rarity and cost mean it’s best to keep them out of close combat whenever possible.

They are most effective firing at massed infantry and archers, but can also inflict serious damage on charging heavy cavalry if given time to react. They excel in sieges (both defense and offense), devastating defenders on ramparts or attackers in courtyards — though this risks costly, irreplaceable losses.

Key weaknesses: they lack shields, making them vulnerable when outnumbered. Small cavalry groups are not worth targeting with arrows in large battles; it’s better to hold fire and deal with them via melee or ignore them until the main enemy force is neutralized. Proper positioning can mitigate cavalry threats.

When facing large numbers of Welnerian Flinger Champions, prioritize disrupting their ranged fire or forcing them to expend ammunition quickly to avoid severe casualties.
