## Character Overview

Crafendil fen Gruffendoc is the High King of Welneria and the leader of clan fen Gruffendoc. He is the owner of Marthunal and the father of Mengus and Corein.

Following the disappearance of his predecessor, High King Aeril fen Derngil, while hunting, Crafendil led the Welnerian tribes to war in retaliation for the Calradic Empire’s crimes against them. Crafendil was responsible for the Welnerian ambush that decimated the imperial vanguard during the Battle of Pendraic. In the aftermath of the fighting, Crafendil came to blows with then‑Prince Raganslav Gundaroving regarding the spoils of war, with the latter labeling the former a coward for taking time to strip the dead.
