## Settlement Overview

Balesk is a town in Nordia, owned by Grand Prince Raganslav of the Gundaroving clan. It sits on the icy waters of Mazopor Bay and was founded by migrants from the Nordlands.

Legend tells that the first Nords who arrived arrogantly demanded tribute from the nearby Vakken and Nordian tribes. The locals formed an alliance and drove the newcomers back to their ships. However, the victors soon quarrelled over the spoils. Then a shrewd Nord named Gundar landed and offered to help them fairly divide the weapons and armour of his fallen kinsmen. The residents acknowledged Gundar’s authority over the region, and his descendants — who intermarried with the Nordians — are now the grand princes of Nordia.

The villages of Marabrot and Safna are bound to Balesk; notably, Safna produces iron for the town’s smithy.
