Imperial Elite Menavliatons are tier‑five infantry of the Calradic Empire.

---

- **Upgrades to:** None
- **Upgrade Cost:** None
- **XP for Kill:** 2048 experience
- **Ransom Value:** Unknown (?) denars

---

## Tactics

Imperial Elite Menavliatons carry Spearmans along with a one‑handed sword. They are vulnerable to archer fire and should be well‑protected by shield units. They should only be deployed once the threat of archers is mitigated or absent. When facing projectiles, shielded units (e.g., Legionaries) should be placed in front of them for protection. Once enemy cavalry charges, they can be brought forward to act as anti‑cavalry pikes. They also serve as effective shock troops, particularly when attacking from the flanks or rear of engaged infantry.
